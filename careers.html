Treatment Room Rental

Practitioner Opportunity

Stillwater Wellness Clinic has a fully equipped treatment room available for an RMT, acupuncturist, chiropractor, or qualified wellness practitioner. This opportunity suits an established professional seeking a welcoming clinic environment with the systems and support needed to focus on client care.

LocationLangford, BC
ArrangementFlat monthly room rental
AvailabilityWeekday mornings or afternoons, plus weekends
Work settingIn person

What the Clinic Provides

  • Electric treatment table and linens
  • Laundry service
  • Jane online booking and payment processing
  • Direct billing access, where applicable
  • Administrative Support when available
  • Utilities included
  • Free on-site parking and access to public transit
  • A modern, peaceful clinic shared with a supportive team

Professional Expectations

  • Provide care within your professional scope and standards
  • Maintain complete, accurate, and confidential client records
  • Communicate with clients in a clear and professional manner
  • Maintain a clean, safe, and welcoming treatment space
  • Manage your own patient check-in and billing
  • Collaborate respectfully with other clinic practitioners

Qualifications

Applicants must hold the registration, certification, and professional liability insurance required for their discipline. RMTs, acupuncturists, and chiropractors must be in good standing with their applicable regulatory college. An established practice or client base is preferred.
