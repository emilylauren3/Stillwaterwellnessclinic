About Us

Stillwater Wellness Clinic is now open as a peaceful wellness destination in Langford, BC, where professional therapeutic care meets a deeply calming environment.

Our Approach

Our care philosophy is rooted in presence, professionalism, and respect for the connection between mind and body. Every detail is shaped to help clients feel grounded, supported, and genuinely cared for.

The Space

The clinic is designed to feel refined without feeling clinical, with a tranquil atmosphere, thoughtful flow, and a quiet sense of luxury from arrival to treatment.

A Note From The Owner

Beginning a new chapter in community care.

With over 15 years of experience serving our community, I am proud to begin this exciting new chapter by opening my own clinic.

Throughout my career, I have been committed to providing compassionate, individualized care in a welcoming and supportive environment. Building lasting relationships with my patients has always been at the heart of my practice, and I believe everyone deserves to feel heard, respected, and empowered in their healthcare journey.

My goal is to create a clinic where patients receive high-quality care in a comfortable setting, with treatment plans tailored to their unique needs and goals. Whether you are seeking relief from pain, recovering from an injury, or looking to improve your overall health and well-being, I look forward to helping you achieve your best possible outcomes.

Thank you to the many patients who have trusted me over the past 15 years. I am excited to welcome both familiar and new faces to this next chapter and look forward to continuing to serve our community for years to come.

- Shawna
